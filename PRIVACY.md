# Privacy Policy — SwiftMind

Effective date: September 6, 2026

SwiftMind ("the app") is a native macOS mind-mapping application developed by Kehong Liu.

## Data collection

SwiftMind **does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data**.

- Your mind maps are stored as plain HTML files (`.swiftmind.html`) in locations you choose on your Mac.
- The app is sandboxed and has **no network entitlement** — it cannot send data anywhere.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs.
- The optional local "agent bridge" is a Unix socket bound to your own machine, used only by tools you explicitly run yourself. It never leaves your device.

## Your documents

Documents you create or open with SwiftMind remain entirely under your control. Deleting a file deletes the data; nothing is retained elsewhere by the app.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
