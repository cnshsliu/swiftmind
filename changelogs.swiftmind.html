    • 2026-09-21
      • feat(canvas): free dragging, never-blank scrolling
        Drag and scroll now share one pan limit: the scaled content bounding box must always intersect the viewport inset by 25% per axis. Dragging lost the old content-center wall (a poisoned-viewport heal misused as a pan limit — this is why you could only reach the top/bottom-most nodes); scrolling, previously unclamped, can no longer strand the map offscreen at any zoom. Zoom changes re-apply the clamp. 5 new core tests. (`8be4f3e`)
      • fix(canvas): coverage pan rule — full content at any zoom extreme
        The 25%-intersection rule only guaranteed the content bbox *touched* a safe rect: at the pan extreme just a 25% band of content remained (rest blank), and the band's size varied with zoom — 放大后滚到最远处几乎全空白. Now the scroll-view rule per axis: content larger than the view must **cover** it (stops flush at the content edge); content smaller stays **inside** the window. Window resizes re-clamp too. (`8d15109`)
